One of the busiest engineers at Bitrise doesn't have a laptop. It lives in Slack. Assign it a Jira ticket or @mention it in a thread, and it reads the requirements, posts a plan, spins up a cloud dev machine, writes and tests the code, opens a pull request, watches CI, and more.
